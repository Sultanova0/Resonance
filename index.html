03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A good question.

A few notes before your first order.

How do I choose an instrument?

Choose a category and open a product card. Read the description, compare the prices and add your favourite instruments to the cart.

How do I place an order?

Open your cart, enter your name and prepare an order. Then open WhatsApp, check the recipient and send the message yourself.

Do I need an account?

No. You can explore the collection and prepare an order without signing in.

Can I pay on this website?

This is a student store. Products and prices are examples, illustrations show instrument types, and the website does not accept payments.
